# Product roadmap

[← Back to Refella](../README.md)

## Product direction

Refella connects engagement, loyalty, and referrals around one customer relationship. Development priorities focus on making that journey more useful across business types and messaging environments.

## Growth priorities

- Broader booking, commerce, and payment integrations.
- More flexible loyalty tiers and campaign controls.
- Staff tipping and payment reconciliation.
- Connected customer identities across messaging channels.
- Multi-location administration and team roles.
- Retention cohorts and experiments to measure incremental growth.

## Quality criteria

| Scenario | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| A customer shares a personal invitation | The friend is attributed to the correct referrer |
| A pricing question needs staff input | The conversation is handed off with context |
| A qualifying transaction completes | One reward entry is created |
| A completion event is delivered twice | The reward balance remains unchanged |
| A customer opts out of promotions | Their preference is respected |
| A business accesses customer records | Records remain scoped to that business |

## Measuring value

Track useful answers, successful staff handoffs, repeat visits, reward redemption, referred transactions, and attributed revenue. Compare outcomes against a baseline to assess incremental growth.
