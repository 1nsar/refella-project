## Project description

Refella brings referrals and loyalty into the messaging apps customers already use. A customer recommends a business to a friend. The friend enters a conversation with that recommendation attached, asks questions, and gets help choosing a service. Refella tracks the introduction and the qualifying conversion so the inviter can receive a reward without installing a separate loyalty app.

The conversation carries the referral context: who made the introduction, the program’s rules, and the customer’s next step. The agent helps the customer act on the recommendation while the backend controls attribution and rewards. The model cannot declare a payment or award itself points.

Our hackathon build includes a Telegram conversation prototype and a browser-facing referral API. The API separates the inviter and referred friend, creates shareable invitations, prepares service requests for explicit confirmation, and tracks demo rewards. A business-owner onboarding flow also prepares referral-program drafts. Our integration approach lets businesses use Refella’s agent or call its referral APIs from an existing agent.

Built with TypeScript, OpenRouter with an OpenAI model, Vercel AI SDK, Telegram Bot API, Zod, and Vitest. The demo uses fictional businesses and simulated paid visits; real bookings, payments, and additional messaging channels require further integration.
